ADVANCING RESEARCH AND PUBLIC DIALOGUE ON DEMOCRACY IN THE DIGITAL AGE.

The Institute for Democracy and Digital Society explores how digital technologies shape democratic systems, public discourse, and governance. We bring together research, policy, and public engagement to address the challenges and opportunities of the digital age, with a particular focus on AI and digital transformation.

About

A research institute grounded in democracy, informed by digital and societal change.

We conduct applied research, advise policymakers, and develop tools for democratic institutions navigating the transformations of AI, media, and everyday digital life. Our work spans digital governance, platform accountability, AI ethics, sociocultural impacts, and participatory policy design.

Research & Foresight

Mapping political, social, and cultural dynamics; scenario planning; and data-driven insights to anticipate emerging challenges in digital society.

Policy Design

Translating evidence into frameworks that safeguard rights, transparency, and accountability in politics, media, and digital platforms.

Civic Capacity

Training programs, toolkits, and public engagement initiatives that empower inclusive participation and strengthen democratic life in the digital era.
